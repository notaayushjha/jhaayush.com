Privacy policy

Police Chase

Android game by Ayush · package com.ayush.policechase · effective 18 October 2026

The short version

  • We never ask for your name, email or phone number. An account is optional and only needs a username and password.
  • Your coins, cars, upgrades and best score are saved on your phone and backed up to the cloud so you can get them back.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.

Who we are

Police Chase is made by Ayush, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it collect when you play, and the choices you have.

Your progress and cloud backup

To keep your progress, the game saves your coins, unlocked cars, paint and upgrade choices, best score and whether you bought "No Ads". This is stored on your phone and also backed up with Unity Cloud Save, so you can get it back if you delete the game or change phones.

When you first open the game, Unity Authentication gives you a random guest player ID. Your backup is linked to that ID. It contains only game progress, nothing that identifies you.

Accounts (optional)

You can create an account with a username and password to restore your progress on another install. You choose the username; please don't use your real name or email address. Your password is handled by Unity Authentication and is never visible to us.

You can play without an account. Guest progress is still backed up, but can only be restored on the same install.

Services from other companies

The game uses these services, which collect information under their own privacy policies:

ServiceWhat it's forWhat it may collect
Google AdMob Showing ads between runs and rewarded ads (for example, to revive or double coins) Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads
Google Play Billing Buying "No Ads", coins and cars Purchase details, handled by Google. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase went through.
Unity Authentication & Cloud Save Guest player ID, optional username/password accounts, and backing up your game progress Player ID, username (if you make an account), a secure version of your password, game progress, IP address and basic device information
Unity The game engine the game is built with Basic technical data such as device model, operating system and crash information

Read more in Google's privacy policy, how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Unity's player privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Ads personalisation: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings, under Google › Ads (or Privacy › Ads on some phones).
  • Remove ads: buying "No Ads" in the game turns off the ads that play between runs.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game deletes what's on your phone. To delete your account and cloud backup as well, see Delete your account and data below.

Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Police Chase account and everything saved in the cloud for it (coins, cars, upgrades and best score).

  • Email contact@jhaayush.com with the subject "Delete my Police Chase account".
  • Include your in-game username. If you played as a guest, say so and include the date you last played.
  • We delete the account, its username and its cloud save within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Progress stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game. Purchases made through Google Play stay in your Google Play order history, which is managed by Google.

Children

Police Chase is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Information sent between the game and Google's and Unity's services is encrypted in transit. Progress saved on your phone is protected by your phone's own security.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to contact@jhaayush.com.
