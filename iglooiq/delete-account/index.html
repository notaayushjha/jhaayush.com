What gets deleted

Sign in with the Google or Facebook account you use in Igloo IQ to permanently delete your Igloo IQ account. This removes:

  • your Igloo IQ player ID and its link to your Google or Facebook account
  • your progress backup: level, scores, streaks, boosters, avatar, frames and nickname

This can't be undone. Your Google or Facebook account itself isn't affected. Progress saved on a phone stays on that phone until you uninstall the game. Information Google AdMob collected for the reward videos is held by Google, not by us; see the privacy policy for how to manage it.

Other ways to delete

  1. In the game. While you're signed in, open Settings › Save your progress › Delete account. This deletes your account and backup, then clears your progress on that phone.
  2. By email. Write to contact@jhaayush.com with the subject "Delete my Igloo IQ account". Say whether you sign in with Google or Facebook, and include the email address of that Google account or the name on that Facebook account. We delete the account and its backup within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  3. Played as a guest? There's no account, and your progress isn't stored online. Uninstall the game, or use Delete account in the game, to remove your progress from the phone.

Removing Igloo IQ under Apps and websites in Facebook, or under Third-party connections in your Google Account, disconnects the game but doesn't delete your Igloo IQ account. Use one of the ways on this page.
