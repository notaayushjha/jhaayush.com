The short version

  • You can play without an account. As a guest, your progress is saved only on your phone.
  • Signing in with Google or Facebook is optional. It backs up your progress under your account ID. We never see your password.
  • The game has reward videos from Google AdMob that play only when you choose to refill a booster. To show them, Google receives your advertising ID and device information, but never your account or progress.
  • No purchases and no analytics. You can delete your account in the game, on our delete account page or by email.

01Who we are

Igloo IQ is made by Ayush Jha, an independent developer publishing as TEKTONIC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses when you play, and the choices you have.

Playing as a guest. Without signing in, your progress never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If you use a save code to move your progress to another phone, the code is shown to you on screen and stays with you. We don't store it anywhere. The ads service still runs for guests, as described in Ads and your choices.

Signing in with Google. You pick an account in your phone's own Google sign-in window. Google then gives the game a sign-in token that confirms your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture. The game passes the token to Unity Authentication, which checks it and links your Google account ID to an Igloo IQ player ID.

Signing in with Facebook. The game asks Facebook only for your public profile. Facebook gives the game a sign-in token and your name. Unity Authentication checks the token and links your Facebook user ID to an Igloo IQ player ID. The game doesn't ask for your email address, friends list or posts.

The progress backup. While you're signed in, the game saves a copy of your progress under your player ID: your level, tutorial progress, score and best score, daily streak, daily challenge results, win streak, today's leaderboard score, hints and other boosters, avatar, frames and nickname. Your nickname is the name you choose in the game (a random "Pingu" name until you change it).

The game never asks for your password (Google and Facebook handle it), phone number, contacts, photos or location, and players can't message each other.

03How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in when you open the game again.
  • To back up your progress and give it back when you sign in on another phone, or after reinstalling the game.
  • To delete your account and backup when you ask.
  • To play a reward video when you ask for one, and give you the booster when you finish it.

Your account, email address, name, nickname and progress are never used for ads or passed to the ads service. We don't sell your information, and we share it only with the services that run the game for us (listed below). The other players on the daily leaderboard are computer players made up on your phone, so your score and nickname aren't shown to anyone else.

04Ads and your choices

When ads play. Igloo IQ's only ads are reward videos from Google AdMob. There are no banners and no ads between levels. When a booster, such as a hint or Snow Sweep, runs out, its badge turns into a green play button. Tapping it plays a short video, and if you watch to the end you get one more of that booster. If you close the video early you get nothing, and you never have to watch one to keep playing.

What Google receives. So that a video is ready when you want one, the game starts Google's ads service when it opens and loads videos in the background. Google therefore receives information from your phone even if you never watch a video: your advertising ID, your IP address (which shows your approximate location), your phone's model, operating system and language, the game's ID, and how you interact with the videos, such as views and taps. Google uses it to choose and show ads, count views and earnings, and prevent fraud, under Google's privacy policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We don't receive any of it. AdMob only shows us totals, such as how many videos were watched, never who watched them.

If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. The first time you open the game, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and whether information may be stored and read on your phone for ads. Videos may still play if you say no, but they won't be personalised. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy choices, a link that appears at the bottom of Settings wherever the form applies.

Everywhere else, the videos may be personalised using your advertising ID. To stop that, open your phone's settings and go to Google › Ads (on some phones, Privacy › Ads), where you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or on older phones turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization". You can also manage how Google personalises ads in My Ad Center.

06Keeping and deleting your data

Your account and progress backup are kept until you delete them. Signing out doesn't delete them: the backup waits for you to sign in again.

You can delete your account and backup in any of these ways:

  • In the game: open Settings › Save your progress › Delete account while you're signed in. This deletes your account and backup, then clears your progress on that phone.
  • On the web: go to the delete account page and sign in with the same Google or Facebook account. This works even if you no longer have the game.
  • By email: write to contact@jhaayush.com. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Deleting is permanent. If you played as a guest, there is no account: uninstalling the game, or Delete account in the game, removes everything the game saved. Removing Igloo IQ from your Google or Facebook settings disconnects it but doesn't delete your Igloo IQ account, so use one of the ways above.

Information collected by Google AdMob is held by Google, not by us, so deleting your Igloo IQ account doesn't remove it. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and manage your ad data in My Ad Center.

07Children

Igloo IQ is a puzzle game for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. The reward videos can be personalised, and signing in needs a Google or Facebook account, which those services give only to people old enough under their own rules. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're a parent and think your child has signed in or used the game, contact us and we'll delete the account and help with anything else we can.

08Security

Sign-in and the progress backup are sent over encrypted connections and stored by Unity, which protects its services with its own security measures. Progress saved on your phone is protected by your phone's own security. No method of storing or sending information is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to the minimum the game needs.

09Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, for example by adding purchases or other kinds of ads, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

10Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to see or delete your information, can be sent to contact@jhaayush.com. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we delete them if you ask.
