Privacy policy

Android puzzle game by Ayush Jhaeffective 12 November 2026

On this page

The short version

  • Igloo IQ doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no chat.
  • Your level, score, streak, boosters, avatar and settings are saved only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The game has no ads and no in-app purchases, and it doesn't use the internet while you play.

01Who we are

Igloo IQ is made by Ayush Jha, an independent developer publishing as TEKTONIC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses when you play, and the choices you have.

02What we collect

Nothing. The game never asks for a name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location, and players can't share anything with each other.

Your progress (level, score, daily streak, hints and other boosters, avatar, language and sound settings) is saved on your phone only. It isn't backed up or sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

If you use a save code to move your progress to another phone, the code is shown to you on screen and stays with you. We don't store it anywhere.

03Ads, purchases and other services

Igloo IQ shows no ads, sells nothing in the game and contains no analytics or tracking. It doesn't use the advertising ID.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics, crash reporting and purchase services are switched off in Igloo IQ, so the game doesn't send information to Unity while you play. Google Play may collect information about installs and crashes under Google's privacy policy.

04Children

Igloo IQ is suitable for all ages. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you're a parent and have a question about your child's use of the game, contact us.

05Your choices

  • Feedback: if you send us feedback by email, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. Ask us at any time and we will delete them.
  • Delete your data: everything the game saves is on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it. There is no account to close.

06Security

Progress saved on your phone is protected by your phone's own security. Because the game doesn't send your information anywhere, there is nothing of yours stored on our side.

07Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, for example by adding ads or purchases, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

08Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@jhaayush.com.
