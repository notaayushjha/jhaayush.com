Privacy policy

More Burgers

Android game by Ayush Jha · package com.moreburgers.game · effective 9 November 2026

The short version

  • More Burgers is made for children and families. We don't collect any personal information: no accounts, no sign-in, no chat.
  • Your restaurant, money, levels, skins and settings are saved only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Ads only play when a player chooses to watch one for a reward. They are family-safe and never personalised, and the game doesn't use the advertising ID.
  • Optional purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see card or bank details.

Who we are

More Burgers is made by Ayush Jha, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it use when you play, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We don't collect personal information ourselves. The game never asks for a name, email address, phone number, photos or location, and players can't share anything with each other.

Your progress (money, diamonds, levels, unlocked machines, skins, boosts and settings) is saved on your phone only. It isn't backed up or sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Services from other companies

The game uses these services, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

ServiceWhat it's forWhat it may collect
Google AdMob Optional rewarded videos (for example, a free boost or double reward) Every ad request is marked as child-directed and rated G, so only family-safe, non-personalised ads are shown. AdMob may use technical information such as device type, operating system, IP address and approximate location to deliver ads, count views and prevent fraud. The advertising ID is not used: the game removes that permission.
Google Play Billing Optional purchases of diamonds, Cat Coins, skins and boosts Purchase details, handled by Google. The game only receives confirmation of which item was bought, so it can be delivered.
Unity The game engine, and its purchase service that connects the game to Google Play Basic technical data such as device model, operating system version and crash information

Read more in Google's privacy policy, how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Unity's player privacy policy.

Children

More Burgers is designed for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and the services above are set up for child-directed use as Google Play's Families policy requires. If you're a parent and think your child's information has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Purchases

The game is free to play. It offers optional real-money purchases of in-game currency, skins and boosts. You can turn purchases off, or require a password for them, in the Google Play Store app under Settings › Authentication › Require authentication for purchases.

Your choices

  • Ads: ads only play when you tap a button to watch one. You can always skip the reward instead.
  • Delete your data: everything the game saves is on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it. Purchases stay in your Google Play order history, which is managed by Google.

Security

Information sent between the game and Google's and Unity's services is encrypted in transit. Progress saved on your phone is protected by your phone's own security.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@jhaayush.com.
