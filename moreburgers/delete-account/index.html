There's no account to delete

  • More Burgers has no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud save. We don't hold any data about you on a server.
  • Everything the game saves (your restaurant, money, diamonds, levels, skins, boosts and settings) is stored only on your phone.

01Delete everything the game saved

Either of these permanently removes all More Burgers data from your phone:

  1. Uninstall the game. Long-press the More Burgers icon and choose Uninstall, or uninstall it from the Google Play Store app.
  2. Or keep the game and start over. Open your phone's Settings › Apps › More Burgers › Storage, then tap Clear storage (called Clear data on some phones).

This can't be undone: your progress is gone for good. Things you bought with real money that stay yours for good (skins, the Burger Crown and the Scooter) come back when you reinstall and open the game with the same Google account. Diamonds and Cat Coins you spent or still had are not restored.

02Purchases and ads

Purchases are handled by Google Play and stay in your Google Play order history, which is managed by Google. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which is set up for children and doesn't use your advertising ID. See the privacy policy for details.
